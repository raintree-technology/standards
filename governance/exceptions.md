# Exceptions

Exceptions make constraints explicit; they do not make a rule optional.

An exception record must contain:

- The rule ID
- The exact project, system, release, or time window covered
- Why compliance is currently impractical
- The risk introduced
- Compensating controls
- The accountable approver
- An expiration or review date
- The work required to return to compliance

Agents may propose an exception but may not approve one on a human's behalf. `prohibited` rules require escalation to the policy owner and may not use the ordinary exception process.
