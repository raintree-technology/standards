# User trust

Products must help users make informed choices without concealment, coercion, or manufactured urgency.

## Rules

### FND-TRUST-001 — Represent consequences before commitment

**Level:** required  
**Applies when:** An action charges money, publishes information, deletes data, changes access, starts a recurring obligation, or is difficult to reverse.

Explain the material consequence before the user commits. Place the explanation where the decision is made.

**Verify:** Review the final interaction from the user's perspective and confirm price, recurrence, audience, data impact, or irreversibility is visible before confirmation.

### FND-TRUST-002 — Preserve meaningful choice

**Level:** required  
**Applies when:** Requesting consent, enrollment, tracking, upgrades, or communication preferences.

Choices must be understandable and similarly accessible. Do not make refusal misleading, punitive, or needlessly difficult.

### FND-TRUST-003 — Do not fabricate social proof or urgency

**Level:** prohibited  
**Applies when:** Showing scarcity, countdowns, testimonials, activity, popularity, or demand.

Claims must reflect real, current evidence and must not imply a constraint that does not exist.

### FND-TRUST-004 — Optimize with guardrails

**Level:** required  
**Applies when:** Optimizing conversion, engagement, retention, or revenue.

Evaluate user harm and downstream outcomes alongside the target metric. At minimum consider complaints, cancellations, refunds, reversals, accessibility, and long-term retention where relevant.
