# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-20 · Auto-drafted from repository evidence*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Raintree Standards turns product and operational tasks into applicable requirements, evidence, exceptions, and review decisions.

**What it does:** A governed Markdown library connects task profiles to testable standards, foundations, patterns, playbooks, and machine-readable schemas. Each governed rule states its applicability, strength, reason, verification method, and exception boundary.

**Product category:** Agent-readable quality standards and governance library

**Product type:** Pre-1.0 open-source knowledge system; documentation is CC BY 4.0 and executable assets are MIT licensed

**Business model:** Public open-source standards library. No paid service or certification program is represented.

## Target Audience

**Primary users:** Product, engineering, security, data, operations, content, marketing, and AI-agent practitioners who need repeatable review criteria.

**Primary use case:** Start from a task, select applicable profiles and rules, gather evidence, and report satisfied requirements, exceptions, and gaps by stable ID.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Replace vague checklists with scoped, testable obligations.
- Route a task to the right standards and provider playbooks.
- Preserve evidence, freshness, conflicts, exceptions, and accountable review.
- Give humans and agents a shared vocabulary for completion decisions.

## Problems and Alternatives

**Core problem:** Broad advice and generic checklists rarely say when a rule applies, what proof is sufficient, or who can approve a deviation.

**Alternatives:** External standards, internal policy manuals, checklists, and specialist review remain authoritative where applicable. Raintree Standards composes task-oriented routes and does not supersede law, contracts, provider rules, or domain experts.

## Differentiation

- Profiles compose standards through explicit `depends_on` relationships.
- Rules carry stable IDs, requirement levels, applicability, verification, and exceptions.
- Markdown and machine-readable catalogs support both human and agent workflows.
- Source freshness and independent verification are separate from authorship or generation.
- Provider integrations use manifest-backed capabilities, workflows, and evaluations.

## Objections and Fit

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is this a certification? | No. Audit results are scoped evidence decisions, not certification. |
| Are all documents stable? | No. The library is pre-1.0 and includes draft or approval-pending material. |
| Does catalog validation approve the rules? | No. Structure checks do not replace independent or qualified review. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams seeking a compliance badge, automatic legal approval, or a universal checklist that ignores context.

## Customer Language

No representative-reader interviews outside repository review records are established. Use: applicable rule, evidence, task profile, requirement level, exception, gap, review, and accountable owner. Avoid: certified, compliant by default, complete, universally applicable, and independently verified unless a record proves it.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Plain, exact, source-aware, and honest about maturity

**Style:** Start with the decision or task, name stable rule IDs, and distinguish evidence from inference and approval.
